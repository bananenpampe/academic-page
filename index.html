Matthias Kellner

PhD student in computational materials science · COSMO lab, EPFL

Hi there! Welcome to my personal webpage!

I am Matthias, a fourth-year PhD student in computational materials science. My research interests include uncertainty quantification for atomistic modeling, predicting chemical shifts, and the development of universal machine learning force fields.

Currently I am based in Lausanne, Switzerland, working in the COSMO lab at EPFL under the supervision of Michele Ceriotti. I plan to graduate in 2027. Previously I obtained my Bachelor’s at TU Darmstadt and my Master’s from TU Munich.

When I am not researching I enjoy playing music (I play trombone and guitar), sailing, running and hiking!

If you are interested in my work or just want to chat, feel free to reach out on the socials below or drop me an email at matthias.kellner [at] epfl [dot] ch!
